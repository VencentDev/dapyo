# Business Model

## Free Product Commitment

The app is totally free for hikers and organizers. There are no subscriptions,
premium tiers, paid plans, paywalls, transaction fees, or payment-processing
flows in the current product direction.

All core features are free:

- Create profile.
- Discover hikes.
- Join hikes.
- Create basic hikes.
- Basic activity history.
- Basic XP and achievements.
- Basic social interaction.

The priority is to build participation, trust, safety, and retention before
considering any commercial features.

## Current Scope

Do not add pricing or payment requirements to the MVP or the initial product.
In particular, exclude:

- Paid hiker or organizer tiers.
- Feature paywalls.
- Paid events managed by the app.
- Payment collection, refunds, or transaction handling.
- Marketplace commissions or platform fees.

---

## Deferred Marketplace — Only After the App Is Successful

This is an exploratory idea for a later stage, not a launch commitment or a
current pricing plan. Revisit it only after the app has a strong, active hiking
community, repeat participation, reliable organizers, and validated demand.

If it is ever explored, the marketplace could connect users with guides, tour
operators, transportation providers, camps, and outdoor businesses. The core
social app should remain free, and no marketplace pricing, commission, or
payment model is defined at this time.
