# Hiking Social App — Product Vision

## Product Concept

A social platform for hikers where users can discover, create, schedule, and join hikes with other people.

The core product loop is:

**DISCOVER → JOIN → HIKE → EARN → CONNECT → REPEAT**

The application should not position itself as simply another hiking tracker. Its primary value is helping people answer:

> "I want to hike this weekend, but where should I go and who can I hike with?"

## Vision

Build the social infrastructure for hiking by connecting:

- Hikers
- Hiking groups
- Trails and mountains
- Scheduled hikes
- Activity history
- Reputation
- Gamification
- Only after the app is successful: guides, tours, transportation, and outdoor
  businesses

## Positioning

### Recommended positioning

> **The social network for hiking.**

### Product tagline

> **Find your next hike. Find your people.**

## Core Differentiator

Traditional fitness applications primarily focus on what a user already did.

This application focuses on what a user **plans to do and who they can do it with**.

### Existing fitness flow

Activity → Tracking → Statistics → Social

### Proposed hiking flow

Intent → Discovery → Planning → Joining → Hiking → Tracking → Social → Progress
