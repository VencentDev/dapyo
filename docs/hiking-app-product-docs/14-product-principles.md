# Product Principles

## 1. Participation Over Tracking

The product exists to get people hiking.

Tracking is valuable, but it is not the primary product.

---

## 2. Real-World Activity Over Vanity Metrics

Prefer:

**Completed hikes**

over:

**App opens**

Prefer:

**Repeat hikers**

over:

**Downloads**

---

## 3. Free Community First

The app should remain totally free while the community and network effects are
being built. Do not add pricing, paywalls, subscriptions, or payment flows to
the core product.

---

## 4. Trust Is a Feature

Organizer quality, attendance, reporting, and reputation are core product functionality.

---

## 5. Don't Build Everything

Avoid feature creep.

For every feature ask:

> Does this help users discover, join, complete, or repeat hikes?

If not, it probably belongs later.

---

## 6. Don't Build a Strava Clone

Strava is already strong at activity tracking.

The differentiation is:

> **Scheduled hiking + social participation + community + reputation.**

---

## 7. Build Network Effects

Every completed hike should create more useful data:

```text
Hiker
 ↓
Hike
 ↓
Participants
 ↓
Relationships
 ↓
Trail preferences
 ↓
Reputation
 ↓
Better recommendations
 ↓
More participation
```

This is the long-term competitive advantage.
