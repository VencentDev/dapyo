# Target Users

## Primary Users

### 1. Beginner / Solo Hikers

People who want to hike but do not have friends or a group that regularly hikes.

### Main problem

> "I want to hike, but I don't know who to go with."

### What they need

- Beginner-friendly hikes.
- Difficulty information.
- Nearby hikes.
- Reliable organizers.
- Easy joining.
- Social proof.
- Hiking progression.

---

## 2. Experienced Hikers

Hikers who already participate regularly and want to discover new trails and communities.

### What they need

- New trails.
- New hiking partners.
- Activity tracking.
- Statistics.
- Challenges.
- Achievements.
- Leaderboards.
- Hiking history.

---

## 3. Hiking Organizers

Individuals who regularly organize group hikes.

### What they need

- Create hikes.
- Set schedules.
- Set participant limits.
- Manage participants.
- Announcements.
- Waitlists.
- Attendance.
- Organizer reputation.
- Historical records.

---

## 4. Professional Guides / Tour Operators — Future Users

Future commercial users.

Examples:

- Mountain guides.
- Hiking tour operators.
- Transportation providers.
- Camps.
- Outdoor businesses.

### Future needs, only after the app is successful

- Business profiles.
- Event information.
- Reviews.
- Customer management.
- Analytics.

Commercial marketplace and payment features are out of scope for the free
product today.

---

# User Priority

For MVP:

1. Beginner / solo hikers
2. Experienced hikers
3. Hiking organizers

Later:

4. Professional guides
5. Tour operators
6. Outdoor businesses
