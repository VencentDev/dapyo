# Metrics and Product Validation

## North Star Metric

### Completed Group Hikes Per Week

This metric represents the core value of the platform.

The goal is not simply to acquire users.

The goal is to get users to actually hike together.

# Important Metrics

## Activation

Percentage of new users who join their first hike.

## Participation

Percentage of users who attend a joined hike.

## Completion

Percentage of attended hikes that are completed.

## Retention

Percentage of users who participate in another hike within:

- 7 days.
- 30 days.
- 90 days.

## Organizer Supply

Number of active organizers creating hikes.

## Hike Fill Rate

Average percentage of available slots filled.

## Repeat Participation

Percentage of users who join at least two hikes.

This is especially important.

If users join one hike and never return, the product has not established a strong habit.

---

# Product-Market Fit Signals

Strong signals would include:

- Users creating hikes without being prompted.
- Users returning to find another hike.
- Users inviting friends.
- Hikes consistently filling.
- Organizers becoming repeat creators.
- Users caring about badges and progression.
- Users developing recurring hiking groups.

---

# Questions to Validate

Before building too much, interview hikers and ask:

1. How do you currently find hiking groups?
2. How do you decide whether to join a hike?
3. What makes you trust an organizer?
4. What information do you need before joining?
5. How often do you hike?
6. What makes you cancel a hike?
7. Would you join a hike with strangers?
8. What would make you use a dedicated hiking app?
9. Which features would you pay for?
10. What do you currently use Strava, Facebook, AllTrails, or other apps for?
